Sravan Balaji completed his undergraduate and graduate degrees at the [University of Michigan, Ann Arbor](https://umich.edu). He earned dual B.S.E.'s in [Mechanical Engineering](https://me.engin.umich.edu) and [Computer Science](https://cse.engin.umich.edu) in December 2020 and an M.S. in [Robotics](https://robotics.umich.edu) in December 2021.

Sravan previously worked at [Rivian](https://rivian.com/), [Hughes](https://www.hughes.com/), and [MITRE](https://www.mitre.org/) in various roles across internships and full time employment. Since April 2023, he has been working at [Metron](https://metsci.com) in Reston, VA developing and testing mission autonomy software for <abbr title="Unmanned Underwater Vehicles">UUVs</abbr>.

Sravan's professional and academic interests are in controls, motion planning, robotic perception & manipulation, autonomous & connected vehicles, software development, and GNU/Linux among other things. His personal interests include video games, football, soccer, formula 1, productivity software, collecting physical media, building LEGOs, 3D printing, and self hosting (including this website).
