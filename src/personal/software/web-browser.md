After trying [Google Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome), [Microsoft Edge](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/edge), [Brave](https://brave.com), and [Vivaldi](https://vivaldi.com), I finally landed on [Zen](https://zen-browser.app/). Unlike the previously mentioned browsers, it is based on [Firefox](https://www.firefox.com/). This may be seen as a negative for some because there may be compatibility issues with certain websites, but I'm willing to live with the trade-offs. The customizability, great UI, features, and performance are what drew me to Zen. I'm especially a fan of the vertical tab layout and spaces that let me wrangle my many tabs in a way that makes sense for me.
